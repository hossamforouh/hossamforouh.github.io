Design system

The rules behind the site

Every colour, size and component on hossamforouh.github.io comes from the tokens on this page. The direction takes its cue from Oracle's own design language — a decisive red against warm near-black, wide margins, square-ish edges and a single type family carrying the whole hierarchy. This page is rendered with the same stylesheet, so what you see here is what the site uses.

Colour

One accent, used decisively and sparingly. The red is taken straight from the Forouh wordmark, and it carries every call to action, every section rule and every active state. Neutrals are pulled from the wordmark’s ink, which is what keeps the red from reading as a generic alert colour.

Contrast rules

  • Small text on white uses --brand-deep, not --brand. The wordmark red at 16px measures 4.4:1 on white — just under the 4.5:1 minimum — so a deeper #B0333A carries links and small text.
  • --brand is for fills, large type and rules — places where 3:1 is the bar.
  • Dark theme lightens the red to #F06A72. The same red on a near-black ground would be too dim to read.
  • Colour never carries meaning alone. The availability dot is paired with the words next to it.

Type

One family — Public Sans — across the whole site, which is how Oracle handles type too. Hierarchy comes from weight and size, never from a second typeface. Numbers use tabular figures so columns of dates and figures line up.

Shape & depth

Corners stay tight. Enterprise software reads as precise, not soft, so the largest radius anywhere on the site is 8px and most edges are 2–4px. Depth is carried by hairlines first and shadow second.

Principles

  • Structure carries the meaning. Dates sit in their own column, modules in their own row, deliverables under their own label. The layout should be readable before anyone reads a word.
  • Red is a signal, not a decoration. If everything is red, nothing is. It marks the primary action, the current section and the start of a list — nothing else.
  • Whitespace over boxes. Sections are separated by space and a hairline, not by stacking everything into cards.
  • One type family, five weights. A second typeface would add noise without adding hierarchy.
  • Both themes are designed, not inverted. The dark theme has its own red, its own neutrals and its own contrast check.
  • Accessible by default. 44px minimum tap targets, visible focus rings, 4.5:1 body contrast, and a skip link before the header.
