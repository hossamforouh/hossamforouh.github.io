About

From business requirement to a configured, adopted system

A cement plant, a Kuwaiti bank and a school group have all asked me the same question: how do we buy things properly on Oracle Cloud? The answer was different every time. That is most of what this job is — the modules are the same, the business never is.

I moved into consulting from software development. I founded a delivery startup and built its app and its point-of-sale system myself before I ever touched Fusion. That is why the custom reports and alerts I specify in MD.050 tend to survive contact with the technical team — I have been the person on the other side of the document.

I run workshops and training in Arabic and write the documentation in English, which matters more than it sounds: the people who have to live with the system get it explained in their own language, and the client still gets an auditable paper trail.

The part of the job I care most about is the handover. A system nobody understands has not really gone live, so training and hypercare get the same attention as the setup itself.
