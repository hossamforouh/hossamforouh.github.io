About

From business requirement to a configured, adopted system

A cement plant, a Kuwaiti bank and a school group have all asked me the same question: how do we buy things properly on Fusion? The answer was different every time. That is most of what this job is — the modules are the same, the business never is.

I moved into consulting from software development. I founded a delivery startup and built its app and its point-of-sale system myself before I ever touched Fusion. It is why I could design the Warba Bank integration to Temenos rather than write a requirement and wait for a developer to interpret it, and why the custom reports and alerts I specify in MD.050 tend to survive contact with the technical team.

I run workshops and training in Arabic and write the documentation in English, which matters more than it sounds: the people who have to live with the system get it explained in their own language, and the client still gets an auditable paper trail.

The part of the job I care most about is the handover. A system nobody understands has not really gone live, so training and hypercare get the same attention as the setup itself.

Approach

How an implementation runs

The Oracle Unified Method phases I work through on every programme, and the deliverable each one produces.

  1. 01

    Requirements

    Workshops with the business to capture how the process works today and how it should work on Fusion.

    DeliverableRD.011

  2. 02

    Solution design

    Requirements mapped to module features; functional designs for reports, alerts and integrations.

    DeliverableMD.050

  3. 03

    Master data

    Item master, organisation structure and supplier data defined, cleansed and governed.

    DeliverableMDM

  4. 04

    Configuration & CRP

    Full setup of the modules, then a conference room pilot to walk the business through it.

    DeliverableCRP sign-off

  5. 05

    Data migration

    Master and transactional data loaded with FBDI and ADFDI templates, then reconciled.

    DeliverableLoad & reconciliation

  6. 06

    User acceptance testing

    End-to-end scenarios run by the business, defects logged and closed, final sign-off secured.

    DeliverableTE.040

  7. 07

    Training

    Role-based sessions and user guides so each team can run their own part of the process.

    DeliverableDO.070

  8. 08

    Go-live & hypercare

    Cutover support, rapid fixes on live issues, and stabilising the system in its first weeks.

    DeliverableStable operation
